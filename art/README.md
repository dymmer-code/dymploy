# Logo

Same visual family as the other Dymmer marks: the blue of Dymmer (`#5F82AD`), a rounded diamond on a
black squircle, and the symbol cut out of the diamond in the colour of the background. Here the symbol is two
chevrons: the build rising to where it is deployed.

- `logo.svg`, `logo-1024.png`: the application icon (black squircle, transparent corners).
- `mark.svg`, `mark-1024.png`: the diamond alone, transparent, with the chevrons as real holes: it works on
  any background.
- `app-logo-512.png`: full-bleed black square, for the avatar of the GitHub App (GitHub asks for a PNG, JPG
  or GIF and shows it in a small square).

Two chevrons and not three because it has to read at 24-48 px. The files are generated from the geometry in
the SVGs (a 500 px square rotated 45 degrees with a corner radius of 82, as in the other Dymmer marks).
