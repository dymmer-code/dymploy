# Logo

A rounded diamond in the green of Dymmer (`#00cd97`) with two chevrons cut out of it: the build rising to
where it is deployed. The diamond is the family shape of Dymmer's own marks.

- `logo.svg`: the mark, transparent. The chevrons are real holes, so it works on any background.
- `logo-1024.png`: the same, rasterized, transparent.
- `app-logo-512.png`: on the dark background `#101820`, square, for the avatar of the GitHub App (GitHub
  asks for a PNG, JPG or GIF and shows it in a small square).

Two chevrons and not three because it has to read at 24-48 px.
